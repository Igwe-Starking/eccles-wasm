# Support Eccles WASM

Eccles WASM is built by one developer in spare hours, tested on real boards, benchmarked in the open and documented page by page. It is free and MIT-licensed, with no company behind it and no paywall.

If it saved you a reflash, let you ship plugins safely, or made WebAssembly fit on a chip you thought was too small, your support keeps it going:

| A small gift buys | So the library gets |
|---|---|
| A coffee | One more late night fixing the bug you reported |
| A dev board | A new backend tested on real hardware instead of a mock |
| Test gear | More chips in the validation suite, with logs published |
| Writing time | More examples, more guides, a sharper wiki |
| Focus time | SIMD, smaller builds, faster loops |
